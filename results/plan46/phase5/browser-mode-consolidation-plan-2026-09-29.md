# Browser Mode component consolidation plan (2026-09-29)

## Purpose and boundaries

Consolidate component Browser Mode checks into their active production-route destinations only after adding each unique assertion and scenario constraint to the destination and verifying it. This is a plan, not authorization to delete or change test code. The shared worktree already contains unrelated staged and unstaged changes: implementation must inspect the current diff, touch only explicitly listed cases, preserve all unrelated work, and must not overwrite/reset/reformat another contributor's edits.

The classification contains 41 active component consolidation candidates: 17 safe candidates to consolidate after assertion transfer, and 24 held candidates that remain as-is pending resolution of viewport, interaction, fixture, or destination coverage gaps. The 9 Browser Mode → unit/DOM candidates in the triage are outside this plan. Active production-route `.ts` coverage (105 cases) otherwise stays. The two Phase 5 source-only files contain 31 wrapper cases outside the standard config union; their cleanup does not reduce the active batch's runtime. Their unique assertions can move to active destinations as listed below. Unsafe transfers and observations that do not define a test condition remain held.

## Implementation outcome

The assertion-first consolidation was implemented. Seventeen active component cases and 29 Phase 5 wrappers were retired, 46 candidates total. The active config union went from 211 cases to 194; source-only Phase 5 went from 31 wrappers to 2; the full source inventory went from 242 cases to 196. The two retained Phase 5 IDs are `real-legend-toggle` and `touch-swipe-negative`; the interaction-feasibility source file remains with these wrappers, while the Phase 5 rendering-feasibility wrapper file was deleted. The 24 held component cases, 9 DOM-move candidates, and other active route `.ts` cases were unchanged.

All 12 assertion-transfer contracts in the Phase 5 matrix were added to destinations: B07 #60 zero captured errors after the fixed one-second post-navigation wait; B08 #128 details closed and #156 details open; B10 #138 tooltip hidden after close, #159 close visible after retap, and #188 tooltip hidden after outside touch; B11 #284 tooltip visible after retap; B12 #307 URL/hash unchanged; B03 advanced query/reload, advanced parity anchors, and hidden-series pressed transition plus SVG geometry change; B06 Plan27 #9 positive-size bar rectangle. The 17 exact destination contracts in the matrix were also retained. B08 #99 and B11 #249 remain because their active destinations do not yet establish equivalent purposeful contracts.

The two 375px component overflow predicates now target `[class*='chartWrapper']` and preserve document-root overflow checks. The initially attempted `[class*='chartContainer']` selector matched no element in the active route and was corrected. The MonthlyBoundaryAxis CPI and earnings cases include a visible-first-tick non-vacuity check along with the boundary-label assertions. WebKit cases #120/#127/#142 are covered in `batch4-b-webkit-production-route.browser.test.ts`, not the Chromium route file.
